# Contract: Navigation Behavior

## Outside HARO

- Left group: `Home`, `Projects`, `Contact`, `Book Meeting`
- Right group: `Service`, `Account`
- `HARO` appears under `Service`

## Inside HARO on desktop

- Left group: `Home`, `Profile`, `Pitches`, `Mailbox`, `Journalists`
- Right group remains `Service`, `Account`

## Inside HARO on mobile

- The main mobile drawer remains the primary global navigation
- HARO sections are accessed through a compact local dropdown
- HARO subsection links do not become a full global-header set on mobile
