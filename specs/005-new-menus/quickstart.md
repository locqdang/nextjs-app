# Quickstart: New Menus and HARO Navigation

## Verify non-HARO navigation

1. Visit non-HARO routes on desktop.
2. Confirm the left group shows `Home`, `Projects`, `Contact`, and `Book Meeting`.
3. Confirm the right group shows `Service` and `Account`.
4. Confirm `HARO` appears under `Service`.
5. Confirm `Account` shows the correct logged-out or logged-in actions.

## Verify HARO desktop navigation

1. Visit HARO on desktop.
2. Confirm the left group changes to `Home`, `Profile`, `Pitches`, `Mailbox`, and `Journalists`.
3. Confirm the right group still shows `Service` and `Account`.

## Verify HARO mobile navigation

1. Visit HARO on mobile.
2. Confirm the global mobile drawer remains the main site navigation.
3. Confirm a compact local HARO switcher appears.
4. Confirm it includes `Profile`, `Pitches`, `Mailbox`, and `Journalists`.
